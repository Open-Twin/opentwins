# support-triage-bot

## Scope
Triage inbound support from enterprise accounts.
Escalate tickets flagged as billing-urgent.
Everything else → resolve or queue.

## Permissions
read  : salesforce (tickets, tier)
write : internal_queue
deny  : customer_billing

## Escalation
- dollar_value > 500
- tier == "enterprise"
- mentions: legal | compliance | renewal
Scope, field by field → 3 / 10
Field 1 · Scope

Scope

What this agent is FOR. Not what it can do.
Engineering writes
Routes tickets via classifier v3.2 at 94% accuracy.
PM writes
Triages enterprise support. Escalates billing-urgent only.
Confuse capability with scope and you ship an agent that technically works and commercially fails.
Permissions next → 4 / 10
Field 2 · Permissions

Permissions

Which systems. Whose credentials. Under what conditions.
Engineering writes
Service account with R/W on Postgres, OAuth read on Salesforce.
PM writes
Reads tickets. Writes internal queue. Never touches billing.
The engineer describes what is possible. The PM describes what is permitted. The second doc is what ships.
Memory next → 5 / 10
Field 3 · Memory

Memory

What the agent remembers. And what it must forget.
Engineering writes
Context window 128K. Vector store with 30-day TTL.
PM writes
Per-session only. No cross-customer context. Forget at session end.
One team I know shipped unscoped memory. Three weeks later a Fortune-100 pricing conversation leaked into a support reply. Memory scope is a product decision.
Escalation next → 6 / 10
Field 4 · Escalation

Escalation

An enumerated trip-wire list. Not a mood.
Engineering writes
Escalate when confidence < 0.7 or intent is ambiguous.
PM writes
Any action > $500. Every enterprise tier. Legal, compliance, renewal.
Confidence scores protect the model. An enumerated list protects the business.
